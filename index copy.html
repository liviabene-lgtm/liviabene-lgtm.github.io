THE FIND YOUR IMPACT MISSION ↗
Why this exists

A little less searching.
A lot more doing.

Find Your Impact brings volunteer opportunities and causes into one searchable directory, with special attention to the things teens need to know: age eligibility, online versus in-person options, skills, and location.

The goal isn't to tell you what you should care about. It's to make it easier to act on what you already do.

A note from the founder

I made the site I wish I'd had.

Hi! I'm Livia, a high school student in the Boston area.

During my freshman year, I got involved with a nonprofit volunteer organization. It ended up being one of the most rewarding—and genuinely fun—parts of my year. I met people who cared about the same things I did, and I wanted to find more ways to help.

But the more I looked, the more complicated it seemed. I had trouble finding opportunities that welcomed teenagers, and my friends were running into the same problem. Important details, especially age restrictions, were scattered across different websites or difficult to find.

That's why I started Find Your Impact: a place to bring those possibilities together and make exploring them simpler. Whether you're looking for your first volunteer experience or a new cause to support, I hope it helps you discover something meaningful.

Making a difference shouldn't depend on knowing where to look.

How it works

Three steps to getting involved.

01 / EXPLORE

Follow your interests.

Search by cause, organization, skill, or type of project. You don't need to know exactly what you're looking for yet.

02 / FILTER

Find your fit.

Use age, location, format, and skills to narrow the options. Unconfirmed eligibility is flagged, not guessed.

03 / CONNECT

Check the details.

Visit the official organization's page to confirm current openings, supervision requirements, and how to apply.

YOUR NEXT STEP STARTS HERE

Find something worth showing up for.

Browse the opportunities, see what sounds interesting, and take it from there.
