How to use the directory

Here's how to find your fit.

  1. Start with a word or interest. Search an organization, cause, skill, or project type (for example, “cancer,” “animals,” or “transcription”).
  2. Choose Volunteer or Donate. These are separate actions. A general donation option does not mean the organization accepts teen volunteers.
  3. Narrow down your options. Combine format, location, cause, skill, and age. When you enter an age, the site initially shows only listings with a stated minimum age at or below yours. Turn on “unconfirmed ages” to broaden your search.
  4. Read the actual listing. Open “More details” for commitment, supervision, description, and the official source. “Documented” describes a published role or project—not a confirmed vacancy today. “Research / ask” means the role is a lead requiring verification.
  5. Check with the organization. Confirm whether the opportunity is running, whether it accepts your age, how to register, whether an adult must attend, and whether your school will count the hours.
  6. Keep track. Tap ☆ to save an entry in this browser. Saved entries stay on your device, not in an account; clearing browser data or using another device may erase them.
Safety and privacy: If you're under 18, involve a parent, guardian, or another trusted adult when appropriate. Use official signup sites, not unsolicited messages. Avoid sharing your address or personal details in public. This site doesn't collect registrations or donations.
