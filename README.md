I'm Livia, a high school student in the Boston area.</p><p>During my freshman year, I joined a nonprofit volunteer organization. I loved the experience: it was fun, meaningful, and a chance to meet people who cared about making a difference. I wanted to do more.</p><p>But as I searched for new opportunities and talked with my friends, I noticed the same problem coming up again and again. Finding a place to volunteer—especially one that actually welcomed teenagers—wasn't nearly as straightforward as it should have been. Information was scattered across websites, and age requirements were often hard to find.</p><p>So I started Find Your Impact. My goal is to bring opportunities together in one place, make them easier to explore, and help other teenagers discover causes and communities they care about. Whether it's your first volunteer experience or your tenth, I hope this site makes taking the next step feel a little less intimidating.</p><p><strong>Because the hardest part of helping shouldn't be finding a way to help.</strong></p><a href="explore.html" class="btn btn-dark">Explore the directory ↗</a></div></div></section>
